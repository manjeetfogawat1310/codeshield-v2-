Burst errors & SECDED

Compare single-error correction and double-error detection

Extended Hamming (8,4)

Adds an overall parity bit to Hamming (7,4), enabling SECDED: single-error correction and double-error detection.

SECDED
Positions 1–7 are Hamming bits; position 8 is overall parity.
Choose a message and inject a burst to inspect SECDED behaviour.
Why this matters

Hamming (7,4) always tries to correct a non-zero syndrome as if it came from one flipped bit. Extended Hamming uses the overall parity check to distinguish a single-bit error from a double-bit error. Three or more errors are not guaranteed to be classified correctly.
